7 KEY ELEMENTS OF A GREAT AI PROMPT

AI ফটো প্রম্পটের ৭টি মৌলিক উপাদান ও নিয়মাবলি

1. Subject (বিষয়বস্তু ও ফোকাস)

Who or what is the focus of the image?

Be as clear as possible and avoid vague or confusing language. Includes character identity, anatomical hourglass build, saree & outfit drape, complete jewellery sets, hairstyle, makeup, and fixed traditional details (Sindoor, Shakha, Mehendi, Alta).

2. Action (অ্যাকশন ও অঙ্গভঙ্গি)

What is happening in the image?

Use verbs and details to provide context. The more descriptive, the better. Includes Kathak classical dance mudras, flowing saree twirls, rhythmic steps, and emotive glances.

3. Style (ভিজ্যুয়াল স্টাইল ও মাধ্যম)

Aesthetic medium or format

Specify whether you want a photorealistic image, cinematic film capture, or hyper-realistic masterpiece for optimal AI diffusion clarity.

4. Background (পরিবেশ ও স্থান)

Set the scene and location

Describe the environment, time of day, and architecture. Choose from 100 realistic locations like royal palaces, Thakur Dalan, ghats, mustard fields, or ballroom dance floors.

5. Color & Lighting (রঙের প্যালেট ও আলো)

Color palette and lighting capture

Set overall tonal harmony (royal jewel tones, golden hour warmth, cinematic amber/teal) and lighting setup (soft golden hour, softbox key light, volumetric sunbeams).

6. Framing (ক্যামেরা ফ্রেমিং ও কম্পোজিশন)

Shot distance & aspect ratio

Full body wide angle shot, head-to-toe with 15% headroom margin above head, centered composition, and 9:16 portrait aspect ratio for motion clarity.

7. Mood (মুড ও সামগ্রিক আবহ)

How do you want the image to feel?

Define emotional response: festive celebration, magical & dreamlike, tranquil & serene, royal majesty, or romantic grandeur.
